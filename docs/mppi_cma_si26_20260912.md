## Measurement and search

Four simulator placement coordinates spanning the course are compared against
stationary cross-domain V2X observations. Current fit maximum component residual
is 0.00394 m. No stale calibration is silently accepted. OT world polygon is
from the pinned scene and inspected polygon method. Driving traces use AWSIM
GNSS XY and time-interpolated EKF yaw; measurements remain estimates with that
provenance. A bounding rectangle of the frozen vehicle MeshCollider, plus a
0.1 m conservative margin, judges overlap. This preference is distinct from the
official BLOCK penalty, which also depends on other traffic.

Native result JSON supplies completed laps and crash/wall/over/block events.
Events after the requested finish are excluded. Missing metrics fail evaluation;
they are never replaced by zero. The second lap is the flying-lap objective.
Priorities: completion and contact/over penalties, OT overlap, flying-lap time.
GNSS/yaw timestamps are aligned before geometric checks. Rank and the controller's
0.6 m/s^2 corner acceleration setting must appear in telemetry.

The family consists of 16 periodic cubic B-spline lateral offsets in metres.
The original CSV heading convention is retained with the tangent change added.
The global reference is optimized while the current MPPI may modify the executed
line; only measured execution is scored. This is a solo reference-line search,
not an optimization of dynamic overtaking decisions.

Initial finite budget: 3 generations x 6 CMA-ES candidates per condition, bounded
seed-path checks, then repeated checks of the incumbent and chosen candidate.
Each simulation has a 260 s simulated timeout and 360 s wall timeout; the entire
search is bounded to 2 hours and at most 48 driving evaluations, excluding the
failed infrastructure startup and the stationary coordinate probe. No unbounded
optimization or automatic extension is enabled. No production reference is replaced.

## Automatic checkpoint-continuing search

`continuous.py` continues the actual saved CMA distribution, covariance,
evolution paths, and RNG state from the completed refinement. It never
reinitializes CMA around a selected CSV. The initial checkpoints and study state
are hashed and preserved. Each subsequent round copies its predecessor's
committed checkpoints and advances three more generations per condition, then
performs the same four candidate / four incumbent comparisons. A round without
a promotion still advances to the next round automatically.

The initial campaign admits new evaluations for three hours, with an additional
ceiling of six rounds (384 vehicle evaluations, at most 144 new candidates per
condition). An already admitted batch drains under its existing simulator
timeouts after the admission deadline. Every round uses 64 vehicle evaluations;
normal remains four distinct D1-start ghosts in one course, and leader remains
four concurrent independent rank-1 simulations. Each generation has eight
individual CSVs evaluated as two groups of four. AWSIM and controllers are
restarted per group, preserving the existing evaluation conditions.

Campaign files live in `continuous/`; individual round states/checkpoints are
in `continuous/round-NNN/`. Candidate names begin with `c3-rNN-`. After each
completed round, selected references, paired settings, repeated-run evidence,
and route plots are exported automatically to `continuous/results/round-NNN/`.
`continuous/latest_result.json` identifies the latest closed comparison.
The dashboard retains the latest validated selection while a later generation
is still being tested. Evaluation failure remains a candidate score; missing
telemetry, child-process failures, or failed incumbent validation stop automatic
continuation and leave a recorded error for inspection.

```bash
python3 /home/si26-pc008/cma_mppi_20260912/tools/continuous.py --hours 3 --max-rounds 6
# Dashboard and RViz follow aggregate campaign progress:
python3 /home/si26-pc008/cma_mppi_20260912/tools/show_live.py /home/si26-pc008/cma_mppi_20260912 \
  --state-subdir continuous --xauthority /run/user/1000/.mutter-Xwaylandauth.V5NQV3
# Stop before the next group; the running group finishes first:
touch /home/si26-pc008/cma_mppi_20260912/continuous/stop_requested
# A later explicit extension supplies a fresh admission window and total ceiling:
python3 /home/si26-pc008/cma_mppi_20260912/tools/continuous.py --resume --hours 3 --max-rounds 12
```

A normal budget/operator pause keeps pending proposals, recorded candidate
scores, and committed checkpoints. Explicit resume skips already completed
evaluations and continues the pending generation. It does not clear recorded
infrastructure errors or start a duplicate child process. The supervisor and
individual round evaluator use separate locks; the evaluator also retains the
existing exclusive experiment lock. SIGINT/SIGTERM requests the same bounded
pause. There is no automatic infinite restart or deadline extension.
