## Measurement and search

Four simulator placement coordinates spanning the course are compared against
stationary cross-domain V2X observations. Current fit maximum component residual
is 0.00394 m. No stale calibration is silently accepted. OT world polygon is
from the pinned scene and inspected polygon method. Driving traces use AWSIM
GNSS XY and time-interpolated EKF yaw; measurements remain estimates with that
provenance. A bounding rectangle of the frozen vehicle MeshCollider, plus a
0.1 m conservative margin, judges overlap. This preference is distinct from the
official BLOCK penalty, which also depends on other traffic.

Native result JSON supplies completed laps and crash/wall/over/block events.
Events after the requested finish are excluded. Missing metrics fail evaluation;
they are never replaced by zero. The second lap is the flying-lap objective.
Priorities: completion and contact/over penalties, OT overlap, flying-lap time.
GNSS/yaw timestamps are aligned before geometric checks. Rank and the controller's
0.6 m/s^2 corner acceleration setting must appear in telemetry.

The family consists of 16 periodic cubic B-spline lateral offsets in metres.
The original CSV heading convention is retained with the tangent change added.
The global reference is optimized while the current MPPI may modify the executed
line; only measured execution is scored. This is a solo reference-line search,
not an optimization of dynamic overtaking decisions.

Initial finite budget: 3 generations x 6 CMA-ES candidates per condition, bounded
seed-path checks, then repeated checks of the incumbent and chosen candidate.
Each simulation has a 260 s simulated timeout and 360 s wall timeout; the entire
search is bounded to 2 hours and at most 48 driving evaluations, excluding the
failed infrastructure startup and the stationary coordinate probe. No unbounded
optimization or automatic extension is enabled. No production reference is replaced.

### Four-vehicle ghost mode

The frozen AWSIM `CommandLineHelp` identifies `--collisions off` specifically as
disabling vehicle-vehicle collisions. `--vehicles 4` was already exercised by
the stationary calibration probe. This does not make four candidates independent:
V2X may still expose other cars to MPPI, and `VehicleHandicapController` applies
the leader fade only to the actual rank-1 car. Four identical leader-handicap
evaluations therefore require isolated simulator instances or an independently
validated override of those interactions. The current finite search continues
with solo episodes; no four-car driving equivalence is claimed.
